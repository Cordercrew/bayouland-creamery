Since 2013

A small herd, raised with patience.

Bayouland Creamery started with a handful of cows and a simple idea: keep the herd small enough that every animal gets real attention. More than a decade later, that's still exactly how we run it.

Our cows graze, get named, get spoiled a little, and give us rich milk in return. When you buy from us, you're buying from a family that mucks the stalls and knows every cow in the pasture.

The herd

Brown Swiss and Jersey — milked and shown.

Two breeds known for rich, high-component milk, and cows we're proud to lead into the show ring.

Brown Swiss

Gentle giants

Calm, sturdy cows with a long working life — and milk prized by cheesemakers.

Jersey

Small cow, rich milk

Famous for the golden cream that makes Jersey butter and milk so good.

Bred with care

Chosen for type & udder

We breed by artificial insemination, picking bulls for strong type and udder traits — healthier cows that milk well and show well.
